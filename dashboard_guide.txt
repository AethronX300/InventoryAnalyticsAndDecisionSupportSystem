             MERIDIAN INVENTORY IQ - QUICK GUIDE

--- 1. BASIC TERMINOLOGY ---

• SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique ID code given to each distinct product you sell (e.g., P-001 for Tea Packets).
• UNIT: A single physical item. If a customer buys 5 apples, that is 5 Units.
• ORDER: A single transaction/checkout. If 1 customer walks in and buys 10 items in one go, that is 1 Order, but 10 Units. And yes, "Orders" counts ALL sales channels combined (Counter + Online + Wholesale).
• VELOCITY: The "Speed of Sales". How many units of a product you sell per day on average. (e.g., 4.1 u/d = 4.1 items sold daily).
• COVER (Days of Cover): How many days your current stock will last before you hit zero, based on your Velocity.
• REORDER POINT: The safety net limit. When stock drops below this number, the system warns you to order more from your supplier.
• VALUE (Stock Value): The amount of money sitting on your shelf right now. Calculated as: (Current Stock) x (Cost of buying it).
• REVENUE: The amount of cash customers have paid you for items over a specific period of time.

--- 2. ANALYTICS GRAPHS ---

• TREND VS PREVIOUS PERIOD (The Double-Line Chart)
  - Solid Green Line: Your daily numbers for the selected period (e.g., this month).
  - Dashed Grey Line: Your daily numbers for the period right before it (e.g., last month).
  - Goal: Visually compare if you are doing better or worse than the previous period.

• ABC ANALYSIS (The 80/20 Rule)
  - Groups products into Class A, B, and C based on revenue.
  - Class A products make you the most money. Never let them run out of stock!

• MOVERS
  - Fast Movers: Items selling the quickest.
  - Slow Movers: Items collecting dust. Consider putting these on sale!

• CATEGORY PERFORMANCE (Bars + Line)
  - Bars: Which category (Dairy, Bakery, etc.) brought in the most total money.
  - Line: Your Profit Margin percentage. 
  - Goal: Look for tall bars with a high line (makes lots of money AND keeps a high percentage as profit).

• VELOCITY VS MARGIN (The Bubble Chart)
  - Bottom axis: How fast it sells. Side axis: Profit margin.
  - Bubble size: Total cash brought in.
  - Goal: Bubbles high up and to the right are your best products (sell fast + huge margins).

• WEEKLY RHYTHM
  - Shows which days of the week get the most orders. Helps predict busy days so you can prepare staff or stock.

--- 3. DECISIONS PANEL ---

• OPPORTUNITY (Demand Surge)
  - Shows products that are suddenly selling much faster than normal (e.g., Demand up +58%).
  - Recommendation: Order a larger batch immediately to capitalize on the hype and maximize profit.

• WARNING (Below Reorder Point)
  - Shows products where your stock has dropped below your safety threshold.
  - Recommendation: Restock before you run out.
